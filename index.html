Soundmark

Rate every album and song from 1 to 10, discover new music by swiping through previews, play a daily "Guess the song" game, and see what your friends think.

Support

Something not working, or an idea? Write to app.soundmark@gmail.com. Please tell us which phone you use and, if you can, add a screenshot.

Your account

  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a link.
  • Delete your account: in the app go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and everything you posted.
  • Report or block someone: tap the ⋯ menu on a review, comment, message or profile.
