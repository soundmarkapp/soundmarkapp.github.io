Privacy Policy

Updated October 2026

Who is responsible

Soundmark is a free, non-commercial app run by Data Bebia as a private person (contact details in the imprint). For anything about your data, write to app.soundmark@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, password (stored encrypted by our database provider, never readable by us), username, display name, bio and profile photo.
  • What you post: ratings, reviews, comments, likes, lists and who you follow.
  • Chats: your messages, the albums and songs you share in them, and album club rounds in group chats.
  • Notifications: your inbox (who liked, followed, replied or recommended something to you), your notification settings and, if you allow push notifications, a push token for your phone.
  • Your daily game scores.
  • Listening data you choose to add: your Last.fm username, the artists you pick when signing up, and the top albums and artists counted from a Spotify or Apple Music data export you upload. The export file itself is read on your phone; only album and artist names with play counts are sent to us to match them to albums.
  • Reports you send and people you block.

What is public

Soundmark is a social app. Your profile, ratings, reviews, comments, lists, follows and the listening data on your profile (top albums, play counts, favorite artists) can be seen by other users. Your daily game scores can be seen by people you follow or who follow you. Chat messages can only be seen by the people in that chat; they are stored on our servers but are not end-to-end encrypted. If someone reports a message, we read that message to decide what to do about it. Your email address is never shown.

Services we use

  • Supabase (database, login and file storage), servers in the EU (Ireland).
  • Deezer, for the music catalog, cover art and 30-second previews. Your phone loads these directly from Deezer.
  • Wikipedia, for background text about albums.
  • Apple (iTunes lookup), to find an album or song on Apple Music for the "Listen on" links.
  • Apple or Google, only if you sign in with them: they confirm who you are and share your email address (with Apple you can hide it behind a forwarding address) and, with Apple, your name the first time.
  • Last.fm, only if you connect it.
  • Expo (USA), to deliver push notifications, which then go through Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android). The notification text, like "Alex liked your review", passes through them. You can turn push notifications off in the app or in your phone's settings any time. Expo also delivers app updates; for that it sees your phone's operating system and a random token, not who you are. Expo is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which allows data to be sent to it in the USA.

When your phone talks to these services, they see your IP address, as with any website.

Why we may use your data

We use your account, posts, chats, notifications and game scores because you need them to use Soundmark (the agreement between us). Listening data and push notifications are optional: we only use them when you add them or allow them, and you can remove them any time. Reports and blocks are handled to keep the app safe for everyone (legitimate interest).

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking or analytics tools, and we never sell your data.

On your device

The app keeps settings (like your theme) and temporary copies of music data on your phone to load faster. Deleting the app removes them.

Your rights

You can see and edit your data in the app and delete your account any time under Settings → Account → Delete account. That removes your account and everything you posted. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to stop processing it: write to app.soundmark@gmail.com. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to a data protection authority.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete it, your data is removed from our database right away; backups are overwritten within a few weeks.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app.
